title: Store network rationalisation for a specialty retailer
---

A specialty retailer with 212 stores is deciding which to close as leases expire over the next three years. Write the assessment for the client's property committee. Sixty-two stores are contribution-negative; of those, 18 sit within 3km of a profitable sibling and 21 are the only presence in their catchment. Closing the 18 recovers EUR 4.9m annually and transfers an estimated 55% of their sales. Recommend a closure list, name what happens to online demand in the affected postcodes, and set the criteria for the remaining leases.
