title: Data retention and deletion policy
---

Write the retention policy covering seven data classes, from operational logs to customer records under statutory retention. Retention periods run from 30 days to seven years; three classes have conflicting requirements between the privacy regulation and the tax code, and the policy has to resolve each one explicitly. Deletion today is manual and inconsistently performed. Cover scope, definitions, the retention table, the deletion mechanism, the audit trail, and the exceptions with their justification.
