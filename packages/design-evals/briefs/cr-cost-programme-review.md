title: Mid-programme review of a cost reduction plan

A cost programme targeting EUR 46m over two years is fourteen months in and has banked EUR 19m against a EUR 28m plan. Write the review for the client's transformation office. Procurement delivered 118% of its target; the property and IT workstreams delivered 41% and 33%, both blocked on decisions nobody has owned. Half the shortfall is recoverable if three specific decisions are made this quarter. Say what to do, name the decisions and their owners, and state plainly what the programme will land at if nothing changes.
