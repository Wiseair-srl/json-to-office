title: Security review of the authentication service
---

Write the review report for the authentication service, covering findings from a four-week assessment. Nine findings: two high (session tokens without rotation on privilege change; password reset tokens valid for 24 hours), four medium, three low. No finding is externally exploitable without a valid account. Remediation for the two high findings is estimated at nine engineer-days. Present the scope and method, a findings table with severity and status, each finding in detail with reproduction and remediation, and the residual risk after the planned fixes.
