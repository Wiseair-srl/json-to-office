title: Pricing review for a regional logistics operator
---

A regional parcel carrier has not repriced in three years while fuel and driver costs rose 22%. Gross margin fell from 31% to 19% over that period on flat volume. Write the pricing review for the client's commercial director. Segment the book: 12% of accounts are below cost, 40% sit within two points of it, and the top 15% of accounts by revenue carry a 34% margin. Recommend a three-tier repricing with a nine-month runway, quantify the churn we should expect at each tier, and state what the operator does if the two largest accounts refuse.
