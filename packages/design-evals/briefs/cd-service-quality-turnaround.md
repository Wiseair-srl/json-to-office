title: Service quality turnaround plan

Build the deck on turning around a service business whose NPS fell from 41 to 12 in eighteen months. First-contact resolution is 54%, average handling time rose 38%, and staff turnover in the contact centre is 44%. The three are related and the deck has to show how. Present the diagnosis, the plan across people, process and tooling, the milestones and the measures. Twelve slides for a management board that has already seen two failed attempts.
