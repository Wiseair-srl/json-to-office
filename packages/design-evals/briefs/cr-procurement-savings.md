title: Indirect procurement savings assessment

A hospitality group spends EUR 96m a year on indirect categories across 340 suppliers with no central contracting. Write the savings assessment for the group's CFO. Spend analysis shows 68% of value in eight categories, 22% of suppliers taking 91% of spend, and price variance of up to 40% between properties for the same item. Recommend the categories to address first, quantify the addressable saving and the realistic capture rate, and say what central contracting costs to run.
