title: Operating model options

Build the deck laying out three operating model options for a group that has grown by acquisition into eleven semi-independent businesses. Option one keeps autonomy and accepts duplicated cost of EUR 14m. Option two centralises support functions, saving EUR 9m but adding a shared-service layer nobody currently runs. Option three centralises only procurement and IT. Present the options on consistent criteria, show the cost and risk of each, and recommend one. Twelve slides for an executive committee.
