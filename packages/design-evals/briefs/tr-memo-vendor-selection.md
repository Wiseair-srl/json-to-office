Write a short technical memo recommending one of three message brokers for a new event pipeline. Requirements are 50,000 messages per second, ordered delivery within a partition, seven-day replay, and an operational burden the four-person team can carry. Option A meets all four but the team has no experience with it. Option B is already run in-house for another workload but tops out at 30,000 messages per second in our configuration. Option C is managed, meets the throughput, and costs EUR 5,100 a month. Recommend one in under two pages, and state what would change the answer.
