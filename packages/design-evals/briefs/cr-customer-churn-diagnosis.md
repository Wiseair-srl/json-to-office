title: Churn diagnosis for a subscription fitness business
---

Monthly churn at a 140-site fitness chain rose from 3.4% to 5.9% over four quarters while acquisition held. Write the diagnosis the client's leadership team asked for. Cohort data shows the increase is concentrated in members joining on promotional pricing, whose twelve-month retention is 41% against 68% for full-price joins. Sites that added a staffed onboarding session hold both cohorts within four points of each other. Say what is actually happening, quantify the revenue at stake over the next four quarters, and recommend the two changes worth making first.
