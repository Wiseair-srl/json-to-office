title: Innovation portfolio review

Build the deck reviewing an innovation portfolio of 23 projects consuming EUR 18m a year. Eleven projects are in their third year with no revenue; four have reached EUR 2m or more; the rest are under two years old. The portfolio is 78% incremental against a stated 50/30/20 ambition. Present the portfolio on two axes, name what to stop, what to fund and what to leave alone, and the reallocation that follows. Twelve slides.
