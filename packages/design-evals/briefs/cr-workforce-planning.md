title: Workforce plan for a growing engineering firm
---

An engineering consultancy plans to grow from 240 to 400 staff in three years. Write the workforce plan for the client's managing partners. Attrition runs at 17% and rises to 26% among staff with three to five years' tenure. Hiring at the current rate fills 62% of the gap; the rest has to come from retention or from changing the mix of junior to senior. Recommend a hiring and retention plan, show the headcount bridge year by year, and say what the plan costs against the current run rate.
