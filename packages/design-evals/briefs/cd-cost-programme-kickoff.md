Build the kickoff deck for a EUR 40m cost programme across five workstreams. Cover why now (margin down 6 points in two years while peers held), the target and how it splits by workstream, the governance, the first ninety days, and what each workstream owner is accountable for by when. The audience is the twenty people who will run it. Ten to twelve slides.
