title: Service model redesign for a public transport authority
---

A regional transport authority handles 210,000 customer contacts a year across phone, email and counters, at a cost of EUR 6.40 per contact. Write the assessment for the authority's executive. Forty-four percent of contacts are three question types that a self-service answer would resolve; the counters serve 8% of contacts at 31% of the cost. Recommend a redesigned service model, quantify the saving and the transition cost, and be explicit about the passengers who lose access if counters close.
