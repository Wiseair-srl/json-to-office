title: Market scan of three adjacent segments

Build the deck presenting a scan of three adjacent segments a client could enter. Segment A: EUR 180m, growing 14%, four credible incumbents, our capability overlap 70%. Segment B: EUR 620m, growing 5%, fragmented, overlap 40%. Segment C: EUR 95m, growing 31%, two incumbents both venture-funded, overlap 85%. Present the comparison, the screening criteria, and a recommendation with the evidence behind it. Every chart states its takeaway. Ten slides.
