title: Organisation design proposal

Build the deck proposing a new organisation design for a 900-person division currently arranged by geography. The proposal moves to a customer-segment structure with shared delivery. It affects 140 roles, creates 11 new leadership positions and removes 7. Present the case for change, the proposed design, what changes for whom, the transition over two quarters, and the risks. The audience is the current leadership team, seven of whom are affected. Twelve slides.
