title: Capital allocation review

Build the deck presenting a capital allocation review for a group deploying EUR 120m a year across four divisions. Returns diverge: division A returns 19% on incremental capital, B 11%, C 6%, D 14%, while allocation has been roughly proportional to revenue. Present the returns by division, what a returns-led allocation would look like, the constraints that make a pure reallocation impossible, and a recommended shift over three years. Ten slides for a board.
