title: Integration priorities after a mid-market acquisition

A facilities-management group has acquired a competitor a third its size. Write the integration assessment for the client's steering committee. Overlap is 31% of customers and 12 of 41 depots. Synergy of EUR 8.2m annually is available, 60% of it in the first eighteen months, but 40% depends on migrating both companies onto one dispatch system and neither party has budgeted the migration. Recommend the sequence, name the six-month milestones, and state what is lost by deferring the system migration a year.
