title: Brand repositioning assessment for a heritage food brand

A 90-year-old food brand has lost 4 points of share to private label in three years while its own price gap widened to 38%. Write the repositioning assessment for the client's marketing director. Consumer research shows the brand is trusted but seen as dated by shoppers under 40, who are 61% of category growth. Two repositioning options exist: narrow the price gap and defend volume, or premiumise and accept a smaller, better business. Recommend one, and say what evidence would change the recommendation.
