Build the QBR deck for a business unit. Revenue EUR 61.4m against a EUR 63m plan, margin 18.2% against 17.5%, pipeline coverage 2.8x against a 3.5x target, headcount 312 against 325 planned. Two of five initiatives are on track, two slipped a quarter, one is stopped. Present performance against plan, the reasons for each variance, what changes next quarter, and the two decisions needed from the audience. Charts, not tables of numbers. Twelve slides.
