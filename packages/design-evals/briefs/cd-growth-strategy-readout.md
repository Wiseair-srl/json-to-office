title: Growth strategy readout to the board

Build the deck for a board readout on where growth comes from over three years. Today revenue is EUR 218m growing 4%; the plan reaches EUR 310m. Three sources: 41% from existing-customer expansion at a 22% attach rate, 34% from two adjacent segments, 25% from pricing. Each carries a different confidence and a different investment. Twelve to fifteen slides, one message per slide, an executive summary the board could read alone, and a chart for the revenue bridge.
