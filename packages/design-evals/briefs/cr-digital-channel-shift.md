title: Digital channel shift for a regional insurer
---

A regional insurer sells 78% of new motor policies through brokers at a 14% commission. Digital direct sales convert at 2.1% of visits and cost EUR 41 per policy acquired against EUR 96 through brokers, but only 9% of new business comes that way. Write the assessment for the client's distribution lead. Recommend a channel mix for three years out, say what happens to broker relationships along the way, and be specific about the year in which the shift stops costing money and starts making it.
