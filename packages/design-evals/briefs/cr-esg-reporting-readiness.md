A manufacturer with 1,400 employees falls into CSRD scope for the coming financial year and has never produced a sustainability report. Write the readiness assessment for the audit committee. Of 82 required datapoints, 34 are already collected for other purposes, 29 are collectable within the year, and 19 need new measurement the company does not have. Scope 3 emissions are entirely unmeasured. Recommend what to build first, what to estimate and disclose as estimated, and what the first report will honestly not be able to say.
