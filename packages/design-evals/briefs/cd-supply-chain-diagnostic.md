title: Supply chain diagnostic findings

Build the deck presenting diagnostic findings on a supply chain carrying 94 days of inventory against a 60-day benchmark. EUR 41m of working capital is tied up; 38% of stock has not moved in six months and 12% is obsolete. Forecast accuracy is 61% at SKU level. Present where the inventory is, why it accumulated, the three interventions ranked by impact and difficulty, and the cash they release. Charts state their takeaway. Twelve slides.
