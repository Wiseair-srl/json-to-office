Build the deck presenting findings from 34 customer interviews and a 1,200-response survey. Three themes emerged: onboarding takes 4.5 weeks against a promised two and drives most early churn; the reporting module is the single most-cited reason to stay; pricing is not the objection sales believes it is, at 11% of lost-deal reasons against a believed 40%. Present the method briefly, the themes with evidence, and what each one implies for the roadmap. Ten to twelve slides.
