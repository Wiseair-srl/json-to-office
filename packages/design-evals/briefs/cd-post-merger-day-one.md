title: Day one readiness for a merger close

Build the deck on day-one readiness two weeks before close. Of 118 readiness items, 94 are complete, 17 are on track, 7 are at risk and 3 of those are blockers: the customer communication is unapproved, one country's works council consultation is unfinished, and payroll for 340 transferring staff is not yet cut over. Present the status, the three blockers with owners and dates, the day-one plan, and what is escalated to the steering committee. Ten slides.
