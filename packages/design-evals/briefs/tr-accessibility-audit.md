title: Accessibility audit of the customer portal
---

Write the audit report for a WCAG 2.1 AA assessment of a customer portal. Testing covered 22 key journeys with automated tooling and manual screen-reader passes. Thirty-one issues: 6 blockers (unlabelled form controls in the payment flow, keyboard trap in the date picker), 14 serious, 11 moderate. Four blockers sit in components from a shared design system used by three other products. Present scope and method, the findings by severity with the success criterion each one breaches, remediation with effort, and the retest plan.
