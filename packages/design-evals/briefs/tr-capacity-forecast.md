Write the capacity forecast covering the next four quarters. Compute demand grew 47% year on year and is forecast to grow 38%; storage grew 71% driven almost entirely by one telemetry pipeline that retains raw events for 90 days. At current growth the primary cluster reaches its committed capacity in month seven. Three levers exist: reserved capacity purchase, telemetry downsampling after 14 days, and moving batch workloads to spot capacity. Present the demand model, the forecast with its assumptions, the levers with their savings and risks, and the decision needed this quarter.
