title: Investment case for a platform replacement

Build the deck making the case to replace a 14-year-old core platform. The replacement costs EUR 23m over 30 months and saves EUR 6.1m a year from year three; the current platform costs EUR 4.2m a year to maintain, and three of the five engineers who understand it retire within four years. Doing nothing has a cost the deck has to make visible. Present the case, the alternatives considered, the phasing, the risks and how they are managed. Fifteen slides for an investment committee.
