title: Supply resilience review after a single-source failure
---

A consumer electronics assembler lost eleven days of output when its sole connector supplier had a fire. Write the resilience review for the operations board. Ninety-one of 260 components are single-sourced; nineteen of those have a qualified alternative that has never been ordered. Dual-sourcing the top twenty by revenue exposure costs 1.4% of component spend and cuts modelled downtime by 63%. Recommend what to dual-source now, what to qualify over the year, and what to accept.
