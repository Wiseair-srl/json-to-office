title: Pricing strategy workshop deck

Build the deck for a half-day pricing workshop with a client's commercial team. It has to teach as well as present: the current pricing architecture, what value-based pricing would change, three worked examples from their own accounts, the willingness-to-pay evidence from 400 customer responses, and the decisions the room has to leave having made. Twelve to fifteen slides, built to be talked through rather than read.
