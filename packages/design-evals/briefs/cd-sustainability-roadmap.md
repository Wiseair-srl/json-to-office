title: Sustainability roadmap to 2030

Build the deck presenting a decarbonisation roadmap. Baseline emissions are 84,000 tCO2e, 71% of it Scope 3. The target is a 42% absolute reduction by 2030. Four levers get there: energy contracting (18 points), fleet electrification (9), supplier engagement (11), and process efficiency (6), leaving a gap the deck must acknowledge. Present the baseline, the pathway, the levers with their cost per tonne, and the decisions needed in the next twelve months. Ten slides.
