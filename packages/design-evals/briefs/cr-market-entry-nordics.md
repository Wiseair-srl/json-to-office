A mid-market SaaS vendor selling workforce-scheduling software to hospital groups is deciding whether to open a Nordic sales office in the next fiscal year. Produce the assessment we would hand the client's board. Market size is EUR 340m across Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Finland, growing 9% a year; the two incumbents hold 61% between them and neither has a Finnish-language product. Entry through a Stockholm office costs EUR 2.1m over eighteen months and breaks even in month 26 under the base case, month 34 under the pessimistic one. Recommend entry, name the three conditions it depends on, and show the payback under both cases.
