title: Competitive response options

Build the deck on how to respond to a competitor that entered our largest segment at 30% below our price and took 6 points of share in two quarters. Their unit economics suggest they lose money below a 22% discount. Three responses: match on defended accounts only, launch a fighter brand, or hold price and compete on service. Present the competitor's position, the three options with their P&L impact over two years, and a recommendation. Ten slides for an executive team that has to decide this month.
